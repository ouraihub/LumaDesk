LumaDesk embeds the original Phosphor.ttf icon font distributed with egui-phosphor 0.11.0.
Source: https://github.com/amPerl/egui-phosphor
The original font bytes and existing glyph mappings are retained; no egui-phosphor runtime dependency is required.
